# Boaz Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026.

Boaz is a web browser for iPhone and Mac. It is built so that your data stays yours: the developer does not
collect, see or sell any of it.

## What Boaz stores, and where

- **Your library** (spaces, pinned tabs, favorites, Today tabs and the Archive) is stored on your device. If you
  are signed in to iCloud, Boaz keeps it in step between your devices through your own **private iCloud
  database**. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it.
- **Browsing history, private (Incognito) tabs and page snapshots** stay on the device they were made on. They are
  never synced or uploaded.
- **Optional sync folder.** If iCloud isn't available you can choose a folder (for example in iCloud Drive) to sync
  through. Boaz writes your library there and nowhere else.

## What Boaz does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

## Websites and searches

- Pages you open are loaded directly from those websites, which have their own privacy policies.
- What you type in the address bar goes to the search engine you choose in Settings (DuckDuckGo by default) only
  when you search. Suggestions while typing come from your own history on the device.
- Site icons are fetched directly from each site, not through a third-party icon service.
- Boaz blocks known ad and tracking requests and, on iPhone, can decline non-essential cookies on cookie banners.

## Device permissions

Websites can ask to use your camera, microphone or location (for example for a video call or a map). Your device
asks you first, and you can change the answer at any time in Settings.

## Importing from Arc and other browsers (Mac)

When you choose to import, Boaz reads the files in the folder you select (such as Arc's data folder or a
bookmarks file) on your Mac. If you also choose to import Arc's cookies, macOS asks your permission before Boaz
reads the key Arc uses to protect them. Imported cookies stay on that Mac and are never synced or uploaded.

## Deleting your data

Delete Boaz to remove everything it stored on a device. To remove the synced library, delete Boaz's data in
Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on iPhone, or System Settings › [your name] › iCloud on
Mac.

## Children

Boaz is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
